# Paradox Design System — Obsidian Flow

The design system projects precision, elite financial sovereignty, and focused serenity. Aimed at discerning digital natives and high-velocity spenders who demand immediate financial clarity, the interface mirrors the disciplined aesthetics of high-end mechanical instruments and vanguard fintech platforms like Cred, Revolut, and Apple Card.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Foundation

- **Dark Elegance & Luminescence:** Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations paired with razor-sharp neon indicators create a high-contrast canvas that keeps financial data readable at single-digit glance durations.
- **Controlled Glassmorphism:** Translucent glass plates suspended across tiered spatial layers, defined by micro-fine frosted rim borders (1px) and ultra-subtle directional gradient glares.
- **Tactile Monoliths:** Interactive controls emulate physical matte-black anodized elements with crisp luminous feedback states.

---

## 3. Typography System

Typographic structure relies on a deliberate duality between geometric presence and numerical discipline.

### Typeface Pairing
1. **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Primary UI & Display): Structural elegance across headlines, navigation labels, and narrative context with geometric punch.
2. **Inter** (Numeric & Tabular Data): Mechanical accuracy for all financial balances, analytics, ledger metrics, and micro-caps badges.

> **Crucial Rule:** All monetary figures (₹ INR balances, transactions) must render with tabular numbers enabled (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`) to guarantee strict vertical column alignment regardless of changing digits.

## 4. Layout & Spacing Architecture

The layout adapts a mobile-first fluid model engineered within strict ergonomic reach zones.

- **Rhythm Model:** Strict 4px/8px micro-grid. Component paddings and margins scale via 8px increments (`space-xs` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-xl` = 24px).
- **Screen Margins:** Fixed 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral safe margin on mobile displays to prevent edge clipping on curved glass panels.
- **Vertical Stack Density:** Dense transaction feeds utilize 12px padding between row items, while top-tier aggregate balance cards occupy self-contained 24px padded stages.
- **Ergonomic Safe Zone:** Primary transaction initiators and navigation toggles stay anchored within the bottom 35% of the viewport, safely cleared by the `nav-height` (72px) plus system gesture insets.

---

## 5. Elevation, Depth & Glassmorphism

Depth is established without dense drop shadows, relying instead on surface translucency, rim lights, and directional ambient glows.

- **Level 0 (Background Canvas):** Base solid `#0A0D14`. Ground zero for non-interactive backgrounds.
- **Level 1 (Frosted Glass Plate):** Background `rgba(18, 23, 34, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border stroke colored `rgba(30, 38, 56, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Hero Cards):** Background `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and internal top-lit stroke (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.08) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.01) 100%)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Bottom Nav):** Background `rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered by a continuous 1px `#1E2638` ring. Ambient glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Neon Glare:** Active credit or threshold warnings project a 15% opacity radial aura of the respective semantic color (`#10B981`, `#6366F1`, or `#F43F5E`) diffused over a 40px radius behind the container.

---

## 6. Shapes & Curvature Rules

- **Master Radius (Pill / Full `9999px`):** Chips, tags, action buttons, dynamic pill tabs, and search inputs implement full continuous curves (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Surface Cards (`rounded-lg` / `24px`):** Large structural tiles, glass expense breakdown cards, and charts implement a uniform `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces (`rounded-sm` / `12px`):** Nested icon badges and progress trackers within cards use `0.75rem` (12px) to maintain visual concentricity.

---

## 7. Component Specifications

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Deposit / Pay):** Anodized finish using solid `#10B981` or `#6366F1` with dark slate typography (`#0A0D14`), 52px height, pill geometry. In active states, projects an 8px blurred glow at 40% opacity.
- **Secondary / Quick Filter:** Pill-shaped glass container (`rgba(18, 23, 34, 0.7)`), 1px `#1E2638` stroke, `#F8FAFC` label.
- **Destructive Action:** Low-intensity translucent crimson base (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`), 1px solid `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)` border, glowing Coral Red text.

### Chips & Category Badges
- Compact 28px height, continuous pill curvature.
- **Inactive:** Bordered by `#1E2638` with muted text (`#94A3B8`).
- **Active:** Filled with `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)` alongside an electric `#6366F1` rim and typography. Contains an embedded circular micro-dot indicator (4px).

### Financial Lists & Ledger Rows
- Minimal vertical separators; entries are compartmentalized by a faint `1px` bottom divider (`rgba(30, 38, 56, 0.4)`).
- **Left slot:** 44px rounded squircle icon container (`#121722`) with minimal vector icons rendered in `#94A3B8`.
- **Middle slot:** Merchant/entity title (`body-md`, `#F8FAFC`) stacked over category label and timestamp (`body-sm`, `#475569`).
- **Right slot:** Tabular currency output (`label-numeric`), prefixed by `+` in `#10B981` for credit or `-` in `#F8FAFC` for standard debit transactions.

### Input Fields (Expense Logging)
- **Background:** `#121722`.
- **Border:** 1px `#1E2638` standard; shifts to `#06B6D4` with a 2px outer aura (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`) upon activation.
- Integrated currency prefix (₹) rendered in fixed-width tabular sizing.

### Floating Bottom Navigation Bar
- Monolithic floating glass dock suspended 16px above the home indicator.
- Houses 4–5 streamlined minimalist line icons with an active indicator consisting of an indigo neon under-glow dot (4px).
- Central rapid-action logging button elevated via an electric gradient (`#6366F1` to `#06B6D4`).
